My story ✨

Hello! I’m Sridharsini, a first-year B.Sc. Computer Science student who loves learning how technology works.

I’m especially interested in debugging errors and finding clear, practical ways to solve programming problems.

I enjoy trying different approaches until I understand why something works and how to make it better.

Creativity is also a big part of who I am. I love drawing, experimenting with colours, and making unique designs.

Thread work and handmade crafts let me turn small ideas into something special with patience and care.

I believe coding and art both need curiosity, attention to detail, and the confidence to try again.

My goal is to grow my programming skills, build useful projects, and keep developing my own creative style.

Every new concept, solved error, and finished artwork is another step forward in my journey. 🌷
